Hypothesis Testing Game

An interactive game for practicing hypothesis testing and decision-making under uncertainty. Use sample data and a two-sided z-test to decide whether a hidden population mean is negative or positive, or pass when the evidence is inconclusive. Experiment with sample size, significance level, variability, and penalties for incorrect guesses to explore the trade-offs between statistical evidence, risk, and reward.

Excel Solver Interactive Tutorial

A hands-on slide tutorial on using Excel Solver for linear optimization. Work through a marketing-budget allocation example, translate the model into spreadsheet formulas and constraints, and explore an interactive worksheet and step-by-step Solver setup.
